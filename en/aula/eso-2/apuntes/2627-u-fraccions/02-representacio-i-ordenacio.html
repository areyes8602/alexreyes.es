2. Representation on the real line and ordering

How to place a fraction on the line and how fractions are compared.

Representing fractions on the real line

Procedure

→ The denominator says how many equal parts we cut each gap between two integers into.

→ The numerator says how many of those parts we count from $0$.

Example. Let us represent $\dfrac{7}{3}$: we cut each unit into $3$ pieces and count $7$ of them from $0$.

Each gap between two integers is cut into 3 parts, because the denominator is $3$. Counting $7$ thirds from $0$ lands between $2$ and $3$.

Ordering fractions

Symbols

$<$   means less than.

$>$   means greater than.
