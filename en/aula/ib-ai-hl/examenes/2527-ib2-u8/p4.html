Question 4 · A spiral on the Argand diagram

Rotation and enlargement as multiplication by $0.9\,e^{\frac{\pi}{6}i}$; expression for $z_n$; inequality with logarithms; positive imaginary axis

Maximum mark · 10 marks

On an Argand diagram, the point $P_0$ represents the complex number $z_0 = 6$. Every second the point rotates through an angle of $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ radians about the origin, anticlockwise, and its distance from the origin is multiplied by $0.9$. Let $z_n$ be the complex number representing the position of the point after $n$ seconds.

  1. Write $z_1$ in Euler form and in Cartesian form. 3 p
  2. Write down an expression for $z_n$ in terms of $n$. 3 p
  3. Find the first time at which the point is less than $1$ unit from the origin. 2 p
  4. Find all the times at which the point lies on the positive imaginary axis. 2 p
IB AI HL TANS 1.13

Step-by-step solution

a) The first step

$$z_1 = 6\cdot 0.9\,e^{\frac{\pi}{6}i} = 5.4\,e^{\frac{\pi}{6}i} = 5.4\left(\cos\dfrac{\pi}{6} + i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right) \approx 4.68 + 2.7\,i$$

Euler form 2 marks, Cartesian form 1 mark.

b) Step $n$

Every second the modulus is multiplied by $0.9$ and the argument increases by $\frac{\pi}{6}$:

$z_n = 6\,(0.9)^n\,e^{\frac{n\pi}{6}i}$

Modulus 1 mark, argument 1 mark, full expression 1 mark.

c) Less than one unit away

$$6\,(0.9)^n < 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; (0.9)^n < \dfrac{1}{6} \;\Longrightarrow\; n > \dfrac{\ln(1/6)}{\ln 0.9} \approx 17.006$$

Dividing by $\ln 0.9 < 0$ reverses the inequality. Check: $6\,(0.9)^{17} \approx 1.0006$ is not yet below $1$, while $6\,(0.9)^{18} \approx 0.90$ is.

$n = 18$ seconds

Setting up 1 mark, integer answer 1 mark.

d) On the positive imaginary axis

The argument must be $\frac{\pi}{2}$ plus a whole number of turns:

$$\dfrac{n\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi \;\Longrightarrow\; n = 3 + 12k, \quad k = 0, 1, 2, \dots$$
$t = 3,\ 15,\ 27,\ \dots$ seconds

Equation 1 mark, general solution 1 mark.
