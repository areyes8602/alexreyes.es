Question 2 · Sinusoidal model: tides in a harbour

Parameters of $a\sin(b(t-c))+d$ from high and low tide; inequality with technology; limits of the model

Maximum mark · 18 marks

The depth of the water at a harbour entrance can be modelled by

$$h(t) = a\sin\big(b(t-c)\big) + d,$$

where $h$ is the depth in metres and $t$ is the time in hours after midnight. On a particular day, high tide of $12.6$ m is recorded at 4:00 and the next low tide, of $5.4$ m, at 10:15.

  1. Find the values of $a$ and $d$, and interpret the meaning of each in the context of the problem. 4 p
  2. Find the period of the model and hence the values of $b$ and $c$. 5 p
  3. Find the depth of the water at 13:00. 3 p
  4. A ship can only enter the harbour if the depth of the water is at least $11$ m. Find the time interval, within the first $12$ hours of the day, during which the ship can enter. 4 p
  5. Explain one limitation of this model if it were used to predict the depth of the water a month from now. 2 p
IB AI HL TANS 3.9 NM 2.5

Step-by-step solution

a) Amplitude and mean depth

$$a = \dfrac{12.6 - 5.4}{2} = 3.6 \qquad d = \dfrac{12.6 + 5.4}{2} = 9$$

$a$ is the amplitude: the greatest variation, in metres, from the mean depth. $d = 9$ m is the mean depth of the water.

Each value 1 mark, each interpretation 1 mark.

b) Period, $b$ and $c$

From high tide to low tide is half a period:

$$T = 2\,(10.25 - 4) = 12.5\ \text{h} \qquad b = \dfrac{2\pi}{T} = \dfrac{2\pi}{12.5} \approx 0.503$$

The maximum is at $t = 4$, so $b(4 - c) = \frac{\pi}{2}$ and therefore

$$c = 4 - \dfrac{\pi}{2b} = 4 - 3.125 = 0.875$$
$h(t) = 3.6\sin\big(0.503\,(t - 0.875)\big) + 9$

Period 2 marks, $b$ 1 mark, $c$ 2 marks.

c) Depth at 13:00

$$h(13) = 3.6\sin\big(0.503\,(13 - 0.875)\big) + 9 \approx 8.33\ \text{m}$$

Substitution 1 mark, calculator in radians 1 mark, answer 1 mark.

d) When the ship can enter

$$h(t) \ge 11 \;\Longrightarrow\; \sin\big(0.503\,(t - 0.875)\big) \ge \dfrac{5}{9}$$

Using technology, $h(t) = 11$ at $t \approx 2.05$ and $t \approx 5.95$.

Between 2:03 and 5:57, that is, for about $3.91$ hours.

Setting up 2 marks, both solutions 1 mark, correct interval 1 mark.

e) One limitation

Any reasoned limitation. For example: the model assumes constant amplitude and period, whereas the real tidal range changes over the month with the phases of the moon (spring and neap tides); nor does it account for air pressure or wind.

Limitation 1 mark, justification in context 1 mark.
