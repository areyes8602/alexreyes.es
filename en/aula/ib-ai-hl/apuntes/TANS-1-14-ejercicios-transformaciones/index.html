Transformation exercises

The questions for the exercises we will do in class: transformation matrices, reflections of a triangle and a composition.

Transformation exercises

Questions only: the solutions will be added once they have been corrected in class.

Exercise 1

Find the matrix of each transformation:

a) The reflection in the $x$-axis.

b) The anticlockwise rotation of $45^\circ$ about the origin.

c) The clockwise rotation of $\frac{\pi}{2}$.

d) The reflection in the line $y = -x$.

e) The vertical stretch with scale factor $3$.

f) The enlargement with scale factor $2$ and centre $(0, 0)$.

g) The reflection in the line $y = \sqrt{3}\,x$.

Exercise 2

Consider the triangle $ABC$ whose vertices are given by the matrix $P = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 6 & 10 \\ 4 & 7 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$.

a) Use matrix multiplication to find the image of triangle $ABC$ under the reflection in the $x$-axis, triangle $A'B'C'$. Sketch the triangle and its image on the same axes.

Triangle $A'B'C'$ is then reflected in the $y$-axis to give triangle $A''B''C''$.

b) Write down a single matrix that gives the image of $P$ under the composition of the two reflections. Sketch triangle $A''B''C''$ on the same axes.

c) Show that if triangle $ABC$ is reflected in the $x$-axis and then in the $x$-axis again, the image is triangle $ABC$ itself.

d) Which single transformation is equivalent to the reflection in the $x$-axis followed by the reflection in the $y$-axis?

Exercise 3

A triangle with vertices $(1, 1)$, $(3, 1)$ and $(3, 3)$ is rotated $\frac{\pi}{2}$ clockwise about $(0, 0)$ and then reflected in the line $y = -x$. Let $R$ be the matrix of the rotation and $T$ the matrix of the reflection.

a) Write down the matrices $R$ and $T$.

b) Find the single matrix that represents $R$ followed by $T$.

c) Hence find the image of the triangle after both transformations.

d) From a sketch or otherwise, describe the single transformation represented by $RT$.
