4t ESO · Maths challenges Worksheet 30 Sep 2026

3rd ESO revision

Seven exercises on percentages, volumes and areas of composite shapes to prepare for the test on 5 October. The solutions will be posted later.

Download the worksheet (PDF, in Catalan)

Do the following exercises to prepare for the test on 5 October.

The solutions will be posted later.

Exercise 1

In a school of $480$ students, $25\ \%$ play a team sport, $30\ \%$ play an individual sport and $15\ \%$ do both.

  1. How many students play a team sport?
  2. How many play an individual sport?
  3. How many do both?
  4. What percentage of the school does at least one of the two kinds of sport?
  5. How many students is that?

Exercise 2

A library has $1\,200$ books. $35\ \%$ are novels. Of these novels, $40\ \%$ were originally written in Catalan.

  1. How many novels are there?
  2. How many were originally in Catalan?
  3. What percentage of all the books are these Catalan novels?

Exercise 3

A tank is made of a cylinder with radius $4$ m and height $10$ m with, on top, a hemisphere of the same radius. Inside there is a solid cylindrical structure with radius $1{,}5$ m and height $8$ m that takes up space. Work out the real capacity of the tank in m³. Remember that $V_{\text{cylinder}} = \pi r^2 h$ and $V_{\text{sphere}} = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$.

Exercise 4

A metal part is a cylinder with outer radius $6$ cm and height $15$ cm. A cylindrical hole of radius $2$ cm is drilled right through its centre. Use $\pi = 3{,}14$.

  1. Work out the volume of metal that is left.
  2. Work out how many cm³ of metal were removed when drilling the hole.

Exercise 5

A track is shaped like a rectangle $30$ m long and $16$ m wide, with a semicircle on each short side. Inside there is an area of the same shape, with a $30 \times 10$ m rectangle and two semicircles of diameter $10$ m. Work out the area of the track, that is, the region between the outer and the inner shape.

Exercise 6

A square plate has sides of $20$ cm. In the centre there are two concentric circles: the large one has radius $9$ cm and the small one radius $5$ cm. The shaded region is the part of the square outside the large circle, together with the inside of the small circle. Work out the total shaded area.

Exercise 7

A square has sides of $12$ cm. From each of the four corners a quarter circle of radius $6$ cm is drawn inwards. Work out the shaded area, that is, the part of the square not covered by the four quarter circles. Use $\pi = 3{,}14$.
