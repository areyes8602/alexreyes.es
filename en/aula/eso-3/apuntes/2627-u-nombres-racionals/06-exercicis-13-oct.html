43. Combined operations

Work out the operations and, if possible, simplify the result.

a) $\displaystyle \dfrac{2}{7} \cdot \left[3 - \left(\dfrac{2}{5} + \dfrac{1}{2}\right)\right] - \left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right)$

The order is the usual one: brackets from the inside out, then multiplication and division, and finally addition and subtraction.

First the inner brackets:

$$\dfrac{2}{5} + \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{4}{10} + \dfrac{5}{10} = \dfrac{9}{10}$$

Then the square brackets:

$$3 - \dfrac{9}{10} = \dfrac{30}{10} - \dfrac{9}{10} = \dfrac{21}{10}$$

Now the multiplication:

$$\dfrac{2}{7} \cdot \dfrac{21}{10} = \dfrac{2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7}{7 \cdot 2 \cdot 5} = \dfrac{3}{5}$$

And finally the subtraction. Subtracting a negative is adding:

$$\dfrac{3}{5} - \left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right) = \dfrac{3}{5} + \dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{5}{5} = 1$$
