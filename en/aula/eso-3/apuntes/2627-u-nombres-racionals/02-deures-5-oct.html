Homework (5 Oct)

For the next class: exercises 4, 5 and 2.

The solution will be published on Wednesday 7 October at 16:30.

4. Which set does it belong to?

Say which set each of the following numbers belongs to.

a) $-\dfrac{2}{5}$
b) $\sqrt{4}$
c) $2{,}0\overline{3}$
d) $-6$
e) $\dfrac{4}{9}$
f) $0{,}71$
g) $\dfrac{12}{3}$

5. True or false

Say whether the following statements are true or false and, if false, correct them in your exercise book.

a) All integers are rational numbers.
b) A fraction is the quotient of two natural numbers.
c) Recurring decimals are rational numbers.

2. A company's profit

A company has fixed monthly costs of 15 000 € and daily costs of 350 € from making its product. If in a given month (30 days) the company sold 250 units of this product at 70 € each, what is its monthly profit?

Change one of the figures, solve it again and answer the question. What differences do you find? Does the result make sense? Justify your answers.
