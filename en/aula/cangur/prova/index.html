Cangur Test

An international maths activity promoted by Kangourou sans Frontières and run in Catalonia by the Catalan Mathematical Society (SCM). It is an individual multiple-choice test designed so that students enjoy thinking through reasoning and ingenuity challenges: taking part comes before competing.

How the test works

There is a different paper for each year, though some questions may be shared between adjacent levels. Problems are grouped into three difficulty levels by their point value, and each has five answer options (A–E), only one of which is correct.

Primary (Y5–Y6)
24 questions · 1 h
Secondary
30 questions · 75 min
Points per block
3 · 4 · 5 points
Official scoring. The 30 problems split into three blocks: questions 1–10 are worth 3 points, 11–20 worth 4 and 21–30 worth 5. Each correct answer adds the question's value; each wrong answer subtracts a quarter of its value (−0.75 / −1 / −1.25); a blank scores 0.
