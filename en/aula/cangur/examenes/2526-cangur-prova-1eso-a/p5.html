Question 53 points

The faces of the die

MediumLogic and reasoningSpatial reasoning and folding

A die has six faces numbered 1 to 6, with opposite faces always adding up to 7. The three faces around one vertex add up to 14. What are the numbers on the other three faces?

A 1, 2 and 4
B 3, 5 and 6
C 2, 5 and 6
D 1, 2 and 6
E 2, 3 and 4

Correct answer: A
