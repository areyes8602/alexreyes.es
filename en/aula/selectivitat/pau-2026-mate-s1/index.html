01

Piecewise-defined function — continuity, area and tangent line

Find the values of $m$ that make the function continuous, compute an area with integrals for $m=-2$ and find a tangent line of slope $-2$.

System of three planes — discussion and geometric interpretation

Optimisation (Option A) · Geometry in space (Option B)
