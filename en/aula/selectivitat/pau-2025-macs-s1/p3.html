Exercise 3 · Normal distribution and confidence interval

Standardising the normal variable and building a 95% CI for the mean.

Maximum score · 2.5 points

A few years ago, a farm of Friesian cows dedicated to milk production carried out a study on the weight of its cows and concluded that this variable followed a normal distribution with a mean of 580 kg and a standard deviation of 25 kg. It is given that $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0,1)$ satisfies $P(-1{,}96 \le Z \le 1{,}96) = 0{,}95$.

  1. Compute, with justification, the probability that a Friesian cow chosen at random from this farm weighs between 531 and 629 kg. 1 p
  2. We believe that a change in the type of fodder has modified the mean weight. We have obtained the weight of a sample of 10 cows chosen at random: 569, 575, 611, 581, 583, 614, 589, 555, 566, 571. Find a 95% confidence interval for the mean, assuming $\sigma = 25$ kg. Can we claim that the mean has changed? Justify your answer. 1.5 p
Bachillerato CCSS · Block E — Stochastic sense Normal distribution Statistical inference

Step-by-step solution

Key idea: to compute probabilities with a $\mathcal{N}(\mu,\sigma)$ we standardise: $Z = \tfrac{X-\mu}{\sigma}$. For the CI of the mean with $\sigma$ known we use the formula $\bar{x} \pm z_{0{,}95}\,\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$, where $z_{0{,}95} = 1{,}96$.

a) Probability of weight between 531 and 629 kg

$X \sim \mathcal{N}(580, 25^{2})$. We standardise the endpoints of the interval:

$$Z_1 = \dfrac{531 - 580}{25} = \dfrac{-49}{25} = -1{,}96, \qquad Z_2 = \dfrac{629 - 580}{25} = \dfrac{49}{25} = 1{,}96.$$

Therefore:

$$P(531 \le X \le 629) = P(-1{,}96 \le Z \le 1{,}96) = 0{,}95.$$
The probability that the weight is between 531 and 629 kg is 0.95 (95%).

b) Confidence interval and test for change

Sample mean ($n = 10$ cows):

$$\bar{x} = \dfrac{569+575+611+581+583+614+589+555+566+571}{10} = \dfrac{5814}{10} = 581{,}4\ \text{kg}.$$

95% confidence interval with $\sigma = 25$ known:

$$\text{IC}_{95\%} = \left[\bar{x} - 1{,}96\,\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}},\ \bar{x} + 1{,}96\,\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}\right] = \left[581{,}4 - 1{,}96\cdot\dfrac{25}{\sqrt{10}},\ 581{,}4 + 1{,}96\cdot\dfrac{25}{\sqrt{10}}\right].$$

$\dfrac{25}{\sqrt{10}} \approx \dfrac{25}{3{,}162} \approx 7{,}906$, and $1{,}96 \times 7{,}906 \approx 15{,}50$ kg.

$$\text{IC}_{95\%} \approx [581{,}4 - 15{,}5,\ 581{,}4 + 15{,}5] = [565{,}9,\ 596{,}9]\ \text{kg}.$$

Conclusion: the original mean $\mu_0 = 580$ kg belongs to the interval $[565{,}9,\, 596{,}9]$.

We cannot claim that the mean has changed: the original value of 580 kg is compatible with the sample at a 95% confidence level.
