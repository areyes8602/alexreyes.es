Exercise 4 · Probability — C2EM Congress (Option A)

Total probability, conditional probability (Bayes) and determining the total number of registrants.

Maximum score · 2.5 points

The Catalan Congress on Mathematics Education (C2EM) will be attended by university teachers (10%), secondary-school teachers (50%) and early-childhood and primary teachers (40%). 40% of the university teachers, 52% of the secondary-school teachers and 65% of the early-childhood and primary teachers are women.

  1. Compute the probability that a person chosen at random is a woman. If we choose one woman at random from all the registered women, what is the probability that she is a secondary-school teacher? 1.25 p
  2. Compute the number of teachers of each educational level registered at the Congress, knowing that there are 476 women registered in total. 1.25 p
Bachillerato CCSS · Block E — Stochastic sense Total prob. Conditional probability

Step-by-step solution

Key idea: we use the law of total probability to obtain $P(\text{woman})$, and Bayes' formula to obtain the conditional probability $P(\text{Secondary}\mid\text{woman})$.

a) Probability of being a woman and conditional probability

We define the events $U$ (university), $S$ (secondary), $IP$ (early-childhood and primary) and $D$ (woman). The data:

$$P(U)=0{,}10,\quad P(S)=0{,}50,\quad P(IP)=0{,}40,$$
$$P(D\mid U)=0{,}40,\quad P(D\mid S)=0{,}52,\quad P(D\mid IP)=0{,}65.$$

Total probability of being a woman:

$$P(D) = P(U)\cdot P(D\mid U) + P(S)\cdot P(D\mid S) + P(IP)\cdot P(D\mid IP)$$
$$= 0{,}10\cdot 0{,}40 + 0{,}50\cdot 0{,}52 + 0{,}40\cdot 0{,}65 = 0{,}04 + 0{,}26 + 0{,}26 = \mathbf{0{,}56}.$$

Conditional probability that, given a woman, she is a secondary-school teacher (Bayes):

$$P(S\mid D) = \dfrac{P(D\mid S)\cdot P(S)}{P(D)} = \dfrac{0{,}52\cdot 0{,}50}{0{,}56} = \dfrac{0{,}26}{0{,}56} = \dfrac{13}{28} \approx \mathbf{0{,}464}.$$
$P(\text{woman}) = 0{,}56$  |  $P(\text{Secondary}\mid\text{woman}) = \tfrac{13}{28} \approx 46{,}4\,\%$

b) Number of teachers per level

Let $T$ be the total number of registered teachers. The number of women is $P(D)\cdot T = 0{,}56\,T$. Since there are 476 women:

$$0{,}56\,T = 476 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad T = \dfrac{476}{0{,}56} = \mathbf{850}\ \text{teachers}.$$

We distribute by level:

$$U: \ 0{,}10 \times 850 = \mathbf{85}\ \text{university teachers.}$$
$$S: \ 0{,}50 \times 850 = \mathbf{425}\ \text{secondary-school teachers.}$$
$$IP: \ 0{,}40 \times 850 = \mathbf{340}\ \text{early-childhood and primary teachers.}$$

Check of the 476 women: $0{,}40(85) + 0{,}52(425) + 0{,}65(340) = 34 + 221 + 221 = 476$ ✓

Total: 850 teachers. University: 85 · Secondary: 425 · Early-childhood and primary: 340.
