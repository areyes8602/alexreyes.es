Exercise 2 · Derivatives and extrema — Volatile investment fund

Complete analysis of a cubic function: initial value, maximum, minimum and sign.

Maximum score · 2.5 points

An investor has some money invested in a very volatile investment fund. The value of their investment in euros during a given day is given by the function

$f(x) = \dfrac{x^{3}}{3} - \dfrac{35x^{2}}{2} + 300x + 250,$

where $x \in [0, 24]$ represents the time in hours.

  1. Compute the initial value of the investment at the start of the day and determine the profit or loss after 24 hours. Find also at what time of day the value of the investment was at its maximum and what that maximum value was. 1.5 p
  2. Is there any moment of the day when the value of the investment is negative? What is the minimum value it reaches? 1 p
Bachillerato CCSS · Block D — Algebraic and functional sense Derivatives Extrema

Step-by-step solution

Key idea: to find the absolute maximum and minimum on a closed interval $[a,b]$, we must compare the values of $f$ at the critical points (where $f'=0$) and at the endpoints of the interval. The absolute maximum may be at an endpoint, not necessarily at an interior critical point.

a) Initial value, profit and maximum

Initial value: $f(0) = 0 - 0 + 0 + 250 = \mathbf{250\ €}$.

Value at the end of the day:

$$f(24) = \dfrac{24^{3}}{3} - \dfrac{35 \cdot 24^{2}}{2} + 300 \cdot 24 + 250 = \dfrac{13824}{3} - \dfrac{35 \cdot 576}{2} + 7200 + 250$$
$$= 4608 - 10080 + 7200 + 250 = \mathbf{1978\ €}.$$

Profit: $f(24) - f(0) = 1978 - 250 = \mathbf{1728\ €}$ gain.

Critical points: differentiate and set equal to zero.

$$f'(x) = x^{2} - 35x + 300 = (x-15)(x-20) = 0 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad x = 15\ \text{or}\ x = 20.$$

We study the sign of $f'$:

$x \in (0,15)$: $f'(x) > 0$ → $f$ increasing.   $x \in (15,20)$: $f'(x) < 0$ → $f$ decreasing.   $x \in (20,24)$: $f'(x) > 0$ → $f$ increasing.

Therefore $x=15$ is a local maximum and $x=20$ a local minimum. We check the four candidate values for the absolute maximum:

$$f(0) = 250, \quad f(15) = \dfrac{3375}{3} - \dfrac{35 \cdot 225}{2} + 4500 + 250 = 1125 - 3937{,}5 + 4500 + 250 = 1937{,}5\ \text{€},$$
$$f(20) = \dfrac{8000}{3} - \dfrac{35 \cdot 400}{2} + 6000 + 250 \approx 2666{,}7 - 7000 + 6000 + 250 \approx 1916{,}7\ \text{€}, \quad f(24) = 1978\ \text{€}.$$
The absolute maximum value is $f(24) = \mathbf{1978\ €}$, reached at 24 h (end of the day).
The local maximum (critical point) is $f(15) = 1937{,}5\ €$ at 15 h.

b) Minimum value and sign of the investment

The absolute minimum on $[0,24]$ is compared between the endpoints and the critical point $x=20$:

$$f(0) = 250\ \text{€}, \qquad f(20) \approx 1916{,}7\ \text{€}, \qquad f(24) = 1978\ \text{€}.$$

The minimum value is $f(0) = 250\ €$, reached at the start of the day.

No, the value of the investment is never negative on $[0,24]$. The minimum value it reaches is 250 €, at time $x = 0$.
